Privacy

Last updated: November 2026. The German version is the binding one.

In short: noka has no server of its own, no account, no ads, and no tracking. Your lists stay on your iPhone and in your iCloud. I can't see them.

Who is responsible

Thomas Nagel. For address and contact details, see the legal notice.
Email: noka@thomasnagel.org

What the app stores

noka stores your stores, products, items, and purchases on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on, Apple syncs this data between your devices through your private iCloud database (CloudKit). Apple's terms and privacy policy apply. I have no access to this data.

noka collects no usage data, shows no ads, uses no analytics or advertising services, and does not ask for your location.

Sharing with your household

When you share your household, the people you invite see all of the household's data: stores, products, lists, and who is shopping where, with the first name from their Apple Account. Sharing runs through iCloud. You can stop sharing at any time in noka's Settings.

Backup file

“Back Up Data” creates a file with your data. You decide where it goes. noka doesn't send it anywhere.

Tips

If you leave a tip in noka, you pay through Apple. Apple handles the payment. I don't learn your name or payment details, only that a purchase took place.

Beta testing with TestFlight

During the beta, Apple distributes noka through TestFlight. Apple processes data and shares some of it with me:

  • If you were invited by email: your name and email address. If you joined through the public link: neither.
  • Installs, number of sessions and crashes, app and iOS version, device model, language.
  • Crash reports, which Apple collects automatically.
  • Feedback you send yourself, with screenshots and device details.

I use this data only to find bugs and improve noka. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in an app that works (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I delete feedback and crash reports once they are dealt with, and no later than the end of the beta. More from Apple: TestFlight & Privacy.

If you email me

If you email me, I use your details only to reply and delete them once the matter is settled.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement. I use no cookies, no third-party fonts, and no analytics.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object. Just email me. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia.
