Help and contact

Where is my data?
On your iPhone and in your iCloud. noka has no server of its own, and I can't see your lists.
How do I share with my family?
In noka, go to Settings → Sharing. The other person accepts the invitation on their iPhone.
How do I move to a new iPhone?
With iCloud it happens on its own. Without iCloud: Settings → Data → “Back Up Data”, then open the file on the new iPhone with “Restore Data”.
Something isn't working or is missing?
Write to me: noka@thomasnagel.org. During the beta you can also send feedback right from TestFlight, with a screenshot.
